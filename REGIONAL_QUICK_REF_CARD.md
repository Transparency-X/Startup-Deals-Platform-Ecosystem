- [ ] AWS Activate — up to $100K
- [ ] HubSpot for Startups — 90% off
- [ ] Notion for Startups — 6 months free

### Step 2: Choose One Aggregator
| Budget | Platform | Cost | Why |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| €0 | StartGround (free tier) | Free | EU-native, no FX |
| <€100/yr | SaaSOffers Premium | ~€72/yr | Cheapest paid option |
| €100–€150/yr | Secret | ~€136/yr | Largest catalog |
| €150–€300/yr | FounderPass Lifetime | ~€455 once | Best long-term value |
| Team spend control | NachoNacho | ~€117/yr + €9/user/mo | Virtual cards + deals |

### Step 3: Minimise FX Costs
- Use **Wise Business** or **Revolut Business** EUR account with USD balance
- Avoid bank card FX (~2%) → use interbank rate (~0.4–0.6%)
